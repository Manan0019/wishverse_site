Creating your greeting

How do I make a personalized card?

Choose Birthday, Anniversary, Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, New Year, or Christmas. Pick one of the curated designs and a card finish. Enter the recipient's name, your own name, a relationship and a personal note. Preview the recipient experience before sharing.

Can I write in Gujarati or Hindi?

Yes. Use the language selector inside the greeting studio. English, Hindi, and Gujarati have occasion-specific suggested messages and greeting titles. Your handwritten message is never translated or replaced automatically.

Do I need an account?

No. The greeting creator works without creating a Wishverse account. You can save up to eight text-based drafts in the browser you're currently using.

Links, photos and voice

What does a shareable link contain?

The link contains your card's names, message, special note, language, occasion and visual choices. It is encoded, but not encrypted or password-protected. Anyone who has the full link can read its contents. Avoid including private addresses, passwords or other sensitive information.

Does my WhatsApp link include the photo or voice recording?

No. In the current static version, a Wishverse link carries only text and design information. When you add a photo or a voice message, use the Download memory keepsake (.html) option, then send that file as an attachment. Some messaging apps may block or warn about HTML files; a PNG photo greeting is available as an alternative.

Can I remove a wish after sending it?

Because greeting details are embedded inside the link itself, there is no central saved copy to delete or revoke. People you shared it with may keep forwarding that link or saving the card.

How do I send a photo without sharing an HTML attachment?

Use Save premium PNG in the creator. It exports a standard 1080 × 1350 image suitable for sharing. The voice recording will not be part of the PNG.

Can I print a QR invitation for a greeting?

Yes. In the editor choose Open Share Studio and download the printable QR invitation. The QR encodes the same text-based greeting link. The link can be scanned after the public website is deployed. QR generation runs locally in your browser without sending your text to a QR image provider. Long greetings may be too dense for a reliable QR; use a direct link if the QR option is unavailable.

Can a recipient send a quick reaction?

Yes. After opening the card, the recipient can select a reaction and choose to share a thank-you through their device's share menu or WhatsApp. This is not a chat inbox: nothing is automatically sent, stored, or delivered to the creator without the recipient choosing the destination.

Saved drafts, offline access and privacy

Where are my saved drafts stored?

Drafts are stored in this browser's local storage and contain text and card choices only, not your photo or voice recording. They aren't synced to your phone or another computer. Clearing browser/site data may remove them.

Does Wishverse upload photos or voice notes?

No. This static version processes chosen media in your browser. A Keepsake HTML export places the selected media inside the downloaded file that you control. Once you share it via another app, the app's own privacy rules apply.

Does Wishverse work offline?

On a supported browser, you can install Wishverse and it may reopen its public creator and previously visited pages offline. New guides require connectivity. The offline feature doesn't synchronize greetings or upload media, and service workers may be unavailable in private browsing or some browsers.

Is there advertising?

Not in this version. No Google AdSense code or advertising tracker is included. If advertising is introduced later, the privacy information and any required consent flow must be updated first.

Still need assistance?

Support contact details are not configured in this prelaunch package. The website owner must add a working support method before publishing to visitors.
