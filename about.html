THE STORY / WISHVERSE

For the things words
can’t quite say.

Wishverse is a small creative experiment with a big belief: even a short greeting can make someone feel remembered, appreciated, and loved.

Why we made it

We send so many quick messages every day that special wishes can start to look the same. Wishverse makes room for something slower and more personal: a card chosen for the moment, written for one person, and delivered as an animated surprise.

Simple by design

There’s no login required to create a greeting. You choose a mood, type names and a message, and create a shareable link. The greeting details are carried by the link itself, so anyone with that link can view its contents. Please don’t include secrets or sensitive information.

Made for more occasions

Today, you can create wishes for birthdays, anniversaries, Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, New Year and Christmas. Each occasion has its own original illustration, three visual finishes and three suggested message tones. The recipient can unwrap a surprise and reveal a sealed memory, then create a greeting in return. Phase 6 introduces a Memory Studio: add a photo, attach or record a voice note, and download an offline keepsake file. Standard links continue to share only names, text and the chosen theme. Photos and recordings are not silently uploaded.

Ready to make someone’s day?

Sometimes the smallest surprise makes the best memory.
