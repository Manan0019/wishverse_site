THE IMPORTANT STUFF / PRIVACY

Made with care.
Shared with intention.

This is the privacy information for the current Wishverse prototype, including on-device drafts.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Names and greeting messages

When you enter a recipient name, sender name, or personal message, the website uses those details in your browser to render the card. The initial version has no application database storing your greetings.

When you select a sharing action, the entered details are encoded into the sharing URL fragment (#wish=...). Fragments are generally not transmitted in the HTTP page request to our hosting server, but anyone who possesses the full URL can decode its contents. Older Wishverse links used query parameters (?wish=...), which may appear in hosting logs. Encoding is not encryption. Anyone who receives or obtains the link may decode and read those details. Do not enter passwords, private contact details, or other sensitive information.

2. Sharing and saving

WhatsApp sharing opens WhatsApp's service, where your interactions are governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Copying a link uses your browser's clipboard where available. Downloading a PNG renders the card locally in your browser.

3. On-device saved drafts

When you choose Save draft, the website stores the draft name and your personalized greeting details in this browser’s local storage. Drafts are not uploaded to a Wishverse account or synced across your devices. They can remain on this device until removed from the draft list or until you clear site data in browser settings. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view saved drafts. The website currently keeps up to eight local drafts. The chosen greeting language is included with a draft and with shared card text.

4. Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, as part of operating and securing the site. Our public occasion guides are static and may be requested by search crawlers. The website also requests fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard network request information.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

This version uses browser local storage to keep optional saved drafts and language settings within them but does not set advertising cookies, run an analytics tracker, or display Google AdSense advertising. If analytics or advertising are added later, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data handling and consent choices as required.

6. Retention and your choices

Because the greeting generator does not maintain a server-side greeting database, it cannot revoke a link that you already shared. Recipients may forward or save the link. You can choose not to share the link, and you can close the page or clear your browser history if you no longer want it stored in your browser's history.

7. Photos, voice recordings and offline keepsakes

When you choose a photo or record a voice note, the content is processed in your browser for preview and export. Wishverse does not send these files to a server in this static prototype. Downloaded Keepsake HTML files embed the chosen photo and audio, so anyone receiving the file can view or copy them. Treat these files as personal information and share them carefully. Your browser may temporarily retain downloaded files, and installed apps or your device may back them up according to your own settings. Browser-local drafts store greeting text and design only, not your media.

8. Offline installation and cached public pages

Supported browsers may install Wishverse and use a service worker to cache the public website shell, occasion artwork, and public pages that have already been visited. Personal uploaded media and browser-local draft records are not placed into this public site cache. You can remove the installed app or clear stored website data through browser settings. Any greeting link you visit may still appear in browser or messaging-app history outside Wishverse.

9. QR invitations, sharing applications and reactions

Wishverse generates QR invitations in the browser without sending the encoded greeting to an external QR-code service. A QR invitation includes the same personal text and names as the underlying shareable link and can be scanned or copied by anyone who sees it. Sharing a link via a device's native share menu, WhatsApp, Telegram or email transfers the link and accompanying text to the destination selected by you, subject to that service's policies. Recipient reactions create a message for the recipient to share voluntarily; Wishverse does not store reaction records or automatically notify the sender.

10. Children and responsible use

Do not use Wishverse to disclose another person's sensitive information or create abusive or misleading messages. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external sharing services.

11. Updates

The policy may be revised when the website gains new features. The effective date at the top will be updated.

Questions?

A dedicated support contact is not configured in this initial prototype. A working contact channel must be added before the public production launch.
