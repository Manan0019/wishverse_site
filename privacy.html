THE IMPORTANT STUFF / PRIVACY

Made with care.
Shared with intention.

This is the privacy information for the initial version of Wishverse.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Names and greeting messages

When you enter a recipient name, sender name, or personal message, the website uses those details in your browser to render the card. The initial version has no application database storing your greetings.

When you select a sharing action, the entered details are encoded into the sharing URL. Encoding is not encryption. Anyone who receives or obtains the link may decode and read those details. Do not enter passwords, private contact details, or other sensitive information.

2. Sharing and saving

WhatsApp sharing opens WhatsApp's service, where your interactions are governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Copying a link uses your browser's clipboard where available. Downloading a PNG renders the card locally in your browser.

3. Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, as part of operating and securing the site. The website also requests fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard network request information.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

This initial version does not set application cookies, run an analytics tracker, or display Google AdSense advertising. If analytics or advertising are added later, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data handling and consent choices as required.

5. Retention and your choices

Because the initial greeting generator does not maintain a server-side greeting database, it cannot revoke a link that you already shared. Recipients may forward or save the link. You can choose not to share the link, and you can close the page or clear your browser history if you no longer want it stored in your browser's history.

6. Children and responsible use

Do not use Wishverse to disclose another person's sensitive information or create abusive or misleading messages. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external sharing services.

7. Updates

The policy may be revised when the website gains new features. The effective date at the top will be updated.

Questions?

A dedicated support contact is not configured in this initial prototype. A working contact channel must be added before the public production launch.
